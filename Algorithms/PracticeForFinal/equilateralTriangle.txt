Consider an equilateral triangle formed by closely packed coins similar to the one 
shown in the given figure with the side composed of n coins. Design a brute-force 
algorithm in the form of pseudo-code to flip the triangle upside down in the minimum 
number of moves assume we can slide one coin at a time. Then, find its order of 
growth. 


given n
int i =1, j =n, num_iterations = n-1

while(i != j)
    int k =0 
    while(k<num_iterations)
        move one item from jth row to the ith row
    num_iterations-=2
    i++
    j--


order of growth is n squared since we iterate n-1 + n-3 + n-4 + ....
