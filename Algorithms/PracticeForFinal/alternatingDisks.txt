You have a row of 4n disks of two colors, 2n dark and 2n light. They alternate dark, light, dark, light, and so on. 
You want to get all the dark disks to the right-hand end, and all the light disks to the left-hand end. The only 
moves you are allowed to make are those that interchange the positions of two neighboring disks. Design a 
divide-and-conquer algorithm in the form of pseudo-code for solving this problem and determine its efficiency. 

divide into two halfs
merge the two halfs
pseudocode:
    disk_alternating(disk[0...4n-1], int l, int r)

        if(l==r)
            return
        if(l==r-1)
            if(disk[r]==light)
                swap(disk[l],disk[r])
        mid = (l+r)/2
        disk_alternating(disk, l, mid)
        disk_alternating(disk, mid+1, r)

        Merge(disk, l, mid, r);
        return

    Merge (disk[0...n-1], l, mid, r)
        i = mid;
        while(i>=l and disk[i]==dark)
            i--
        first = i+1
        j=mid+1
        while(j<=r and disk[j]==light )
            j++
        
        last=j-1;
        Rotate(disk, first, mid, last)
    
    Rotate(disk[0....n-1], first, mid, last)
        for i = mid + 1 to last
            pos = i
            while (pos>first)
                swap(pos[i],pos[i-1])
                pos-- 



efficiency:
    let k = 4n
    T(k) = 2 T(k/2)+k
    by master theory k log k
    which is 4n log 4n 
    which is O(nlogn)
